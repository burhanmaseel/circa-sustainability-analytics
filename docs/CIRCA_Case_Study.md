The CIRCA Sustainability Analytics System (CSAS)
The purpose of the CIRCA Sustainability Analytics System is to monitor, record, and analyze sustainability metrics, primarily focusing on solar energy performance and environmental conditions across various organizational sites.
When a new site is commissioned, site details are entered into the system, including its location and total capacity. Physical assets, specifically solar panels, are registered with their vendor information, model details, capacity, and installation dates. The system is designed to be flexible, allowing for the definition of various other "artefacts" (equipment or sensors) and the specific attributes (metrics) they generate.
Subsequent to the setup, the system continuously ingests operational data. For solar infrastructure, daily and real-time production data is captured, recording energy produced, consumed, exported, and imported. For other generic artefacts, the system employs a flexible logging mechanism where sensor readings are recorded with precise timestamps, with each entry capturing multiple attribute values like numeric readings, text status, or quality indicators.
Simultaneously, the system tracks environmental context. Periodically, weather data is fetched and recorded for each site location, capturing metrics such as temperature, cloud cover, wind speed, and precipitation. This allows for correlation analysis between weather conditions and energy generation efficiency.
Later, analysts and facility managers can access the system to view performance reports. The system facilitates the comparison of actual energy production against expected capacity and historical weather patterns, helping organizations optimize their sustainability initiatives and detect equipment underperformance.
